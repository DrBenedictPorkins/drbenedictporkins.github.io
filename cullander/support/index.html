Where do removed photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app. They can be recovered there for 30 days.

Does Cullander upload my photos?

No. Photos stay on your device unless you turn on the optional Local AI, which sends reduced-size copies to a server you enter on your own network. See the privacy policy for the only times the app uses the network.

Why does Cullander ask for full photo library access?

Duplicates and similar shots can only be found by comparing photos across the whole library. Limited access works, but only the selected photos are checked.

What happens to the original when I compress a video or keep only the still of a Live Photo?

Cullander saves a new video or photo and puts the original in the Trash pile. The original is removed only when you empty Trash and confirm. A compressed copy that saves less than 10 percent is discarded and the original stays where it was.

What is Local AI?

An optional setting, off by default. If you run Ollama on a computer on your own network, Cullander can send it reduced-size copies of your photos to write descriptions that Search can match. It starts when you tap Start Describing, stops when you tap Stop, and does not resume when the app is reopened.

What are Keep, Not Sure and Trash?

Piles you sort photos into. Nothing is removed until you open Trash and choose to remove its photos.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no subscription, in-app purchase or advertising.
